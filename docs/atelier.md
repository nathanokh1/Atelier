# Atelier

Atelier is a free, browser-based studio for layered image work and game art. You open it, make the picture or the sprite, and leave with a file. Nothing to install, and your images stay on your device.

**About (short)**

Atelier is a workshop for making images. Bring art in, stack it on layers, move and scale it, paint, blend, and export a clean result — or save the document and come back to it. The same studio is built for game work: sprites, tiles, and the small, precise assets that have to look right at actual size. It runs in the browser. There is no account, no upload, and no watermark.

**About (longer)**

An atelier is the room where the work happens. This one lives in the browser. Atelier is a layered image studio: import, paint, select, transform, blend, and export, with full undo and a project file you can reopen. It was built around the tools people reach for every day — layers, masks, type, shapes, and a canvas that stays out of the way — and it stays free. Alongside photo and composite work, Atelier is a place to make game art: individual sprites, sprite sheets, tiles, and the frames a character or prop needs before it ever hits an engine. Same layers. Same privacy. Your images never leave your device.
